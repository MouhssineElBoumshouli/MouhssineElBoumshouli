### DARE-Bench — flagship research
DARE-Bench asks one question: do more interaction steps make an AI agent more reliable, or just more random? Across **240 reproducible runs over 24 tasks**, giving the agent 5 steps instead of 3 raised measured success from **34.2% to 50.0% (+15.8 points)** — and raised instability too. The result is backed by official rescoring of every run and bootstrap confidence intervals over 10,000 resamples.

### SmartImport — flagship product
SmartImport turns heterogeneous supplier quotations into **completeness-aware, explainable comparisons** instead of blindly comparing raw totals. It combines a React/TypeScript frontend, FastAPI backend, PostgreSQL persistence, deterministic multi-currency cost logic, staged PDF/XLSX ingestion and optional AI-assisted extraction/matching with human confirmation. Synthetic evaluation of the document/AI workflow reached **98.99% aggregate document-field F1**, **100% product-line extraction F1 on 23 tested lines**, and **92.31% top-1 matching on eligible unique-match cases**.
